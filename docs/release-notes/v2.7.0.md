- Camera settings: see and change ISO, shutter, aperture, exposure compensation, white balance, WB fine-tune, Picture Style and quality of every camera from the app
- All cameras row: set a value on every camera at once, or copy the settings of one camera to the others
- The Cameras panel shows which cameras have different settings and which can only be changed on the camera
- The app keeps every camera on single shooting by itself
- Test shot: fires all cameras to compare the photos side by side (not saved); click a photo to enlarge it
- Match automatically (optional): makes the cameras look alike in color and brightness; it can be cancelled
- Cameras panel: drag a camera to change its order; the list scrolls with many cameras
