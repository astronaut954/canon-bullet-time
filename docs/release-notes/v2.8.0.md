- Match automatically: gives every camera the same Contrast, Saturation and Color tone, and matches brightness the way the eye sees the whole photo
- Picture Style options: the ± button next to Picture Style shows and changes Contrast, Saturation and Color tone of each camera, or of all cameras at once (dashed amber when only some differ)
- Use gray card (optional): put a gray card in the scene; the app finds it in each photo (you can click or drag to fix it), measures only the card and makes it come out neutral gray in every camera
- Calibrate with chart: print the Stillpoint chart once to measure how different camera models draw contrast, saturation and color tone; Match automatically then makes the models look alike
- Copy from camera also copies the Picture Style options
- After saving, choose whether to discard the saved sessions from the app or keep them
- Debug mode in Settings: saves the test photos and what Match automatically measured, to send for analysis; turns off when the app closes, keeps up to 300 MB and can be deleted from Settings
- Check for updates looks again when the newest version did not have the file for this system yet
