- Timing: choose how the cameras fire in each session. All at once (as before), one after another (interval, direction 1 → N, N → 1 or there and back, loops), in groups, odd then even, or a custom plan of steps (which cameras, and how long after the previous step)
- A timeline in the Bullet time panel shows when each camera fires; the Advanced button opens the options and the choice is remembered
- A camera can shoot more than once in the same session (there and back, loops); the photos are numbered in firing order, so sequences play back in that order
- Test mode without cameras: open the app with STILLPOINT_FAKE_CAMERAS=9 to get simulated cameras whose shots make numbered test photos
