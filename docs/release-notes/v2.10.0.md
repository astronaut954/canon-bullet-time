- Live view: a separate window for a second monitor or TV with what is happening in the shoot. Countdown ("Ready, aim, Fire!"), the cameras in rig order lighting up as they fire, the countdown between groups, a flash on every shot, the photos arriving and the sequence playing in a loop at the end (speed and reverse loop). Modes Operator (everything) and Audience (just what the person being photographed needs); layout arc, circle or line; window (always on top if you like) or full screen on the display you choose; up to two Live windows at once
- Sound: a sound button in the top bar; group sounds between groups (metronome ticks and the group sound), with sidechain-style ducking so the ticks stay under the group sound; the sounds now play on top of each other instead of cutting each other off
- Timing: new Sweep mode, a fast sweep through all cameras with the interval chosen from shortcuts (0.1 to 0.3 s)
- Playback speed in the session viewer goes from 0.25x to 5x
- Simulated cameras (STILLPOINT_FAKE_CAMERAS) now appear in Camera settings, so Match colors and Copy from camera can be tried without cameras
- The window opens wide enough to show all Timing modes in one row
- Fixes: a crash when loading photo previews; the large photo no longer shifts when entering full screen in a session
- Not yet tested on Windows or with real cameras in this version (tested on Mac with simulated cameras)
